What is a misogi?

A misogi is an extremely difficult challenge you set yourself each year: a tough ride, a long run, a brutal climb, a day you weren't sure you'd finish. It deserves more than a photo lost in your phone.

We turn that story into a model to celebrate your success and remind you that you can do hard things.

Questions.

Where does the terrain come from?

From national elevation surveys wherever they are published: England, Scotland, the Netherlands, France, Spain, Italy, Switzerland, North Rhine-Westphalia and the USA. Everywhere else uses worldwide data, about 30 m between points.
