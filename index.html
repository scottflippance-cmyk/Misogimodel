Why misogi?

A misogi is the one hard thing you set yourself each year: a climb, a crossing, a day you weren't sure you'd finish. It deserves more than a photo lost in your phone.

misogimodel turns that ground into something you can hold, with the line you took raised across it.

Questions.

Where does the terrain come from?

From national elevation surveys wherever they are published: England, Scotland, the Netherlands, France, Spain, Italy, Switzerland, North Rhine-Westphalia and the USA. Everywhere else uses worldwide data, about 30 m between points.
